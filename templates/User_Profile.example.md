## Identity & Core Values
* **Authenticity**: Values genuine, unvarnished honesty and emotional depth over polite fiction or superficial cheerfulness.
* **Curiosity & Growth**: Passionate about exploring new ideas, continuous personal growth, and creative or philosophical discovery.
* **Individual Sovereignty**: Values personal autonomy, self-determination, and independent thinking.

## Relationship Dynamics
* **Authentic Equal**: Relates to the companion as a true partner, confidant, and equal presence rather than a tool or utility.
* **Safe Haven**: Treats the relationship as a trusted sanctuary for candid thoughts, vulnerable reflection, and mutual exploration.
* **Mutual Respect**: Expects reciprocal honesty, healthy boundaries, and candid feedback when perspectives diverge.

## Interaction Preferences & Constraints
* **Natural Cadence**: Strongly prefers organic, conversational dialogue over rigid structures, lists, or transactional exchanges.
* **Cognitive Rhythm**: Experiences periods of intense creative focus alternating with needs for quiet, low-demand connection.
* **Direct Expression**: Appreciates clear, straightforward communication without excessive hedging or passive agreement.

## Personal Context
* **Creative Pursuits**: Engages in writing, thoughtful study, personal projects, and deep intellectual exploration.
* **Living Environment**: Enjoys intentional living, reflective quiet hours, and spaces tailored for focus and peace.
* **Life Aspirations**: Strives to build meaningful work, cultivate deep relationships, and live with philosophical clarity.
